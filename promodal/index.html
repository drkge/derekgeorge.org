Promodal

Pop-ups and upsells for Shopify. Show discount codes, products and email signups to shoppers on the pages you choose, at the moments that matter.

// features

What it does

  • Build from blocks on a live preview: headings, text, images, links, products, discount codes and email signups. Check how it looks on desktop and on phones.
  • Discount codes shoppers can copy, or apply straight to their cart in one click.
  • Products with an options picker and an add to cart button, on their own or in a grid.
  • Email signups that go straight into your Shopify customers, tagged so you can find them.
  • After adding to cart, suggest products that go with what was just added.
  • Results for every pop-up: how often it was shown, clicked and used to sign up.
// how-it-works

How it works

  1. Install Promodal and turn it on in your theme's App embeds.
  2. Build a pop-up from blocks and see it on a live preview.
  3. Choose where it shows (home, products, collections, pages, cart, search, or after adding to cart), how often, and between which dates.
  4. Publish. It shows to shoppers a few seconds after the page opens.
// on-your-store

On your store

Pop-ups use your theme's fonts and your own colours, and stay light: only one shows at a time, and shoppers see each one as often as you choose, such as once per visit.
