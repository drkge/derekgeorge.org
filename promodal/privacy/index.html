Promodal privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026.

Promodal is a Shopify app that lets merchants show promotional pop-ups (modals) on their online store. It is made by Derek George (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information Promodal collects when a merchant installs it, what happens on the merchant's storefront, and how that information is used, shared and deleted.

// in-short

In short

  • We store what a merchant needs to run the app: their store's details, the modals they create, and their settings.
  • We don't collect or store personal information about the merchant's customers (shoppers). Results are counts, with nothing that identifies a shopper.
  • Email signups go straight to the merchant's own Shopify customer list. They never reach us.
  • We don't sell information, and we delete a store's data after it uninstalls the app.
// merchants

Information about merchants

When a merchant installs Promodal, we store:

  • The store's Shopify domain and ID, and the access token Shopify gives the app, so the app can work with the store.
  • The modals the merchant creates: their names, content (text, images, products, discount codes), design, where and when they show, and their status.
  • The store's app settings, its plan and free trial dates, and when the app last saw the storefront working.
  • Daily counts for each modal: how many times it was shown, clicked and used to sign up.

To do its job, the app also reads, but doesn't keep a copy of, the store's products, collections, pages, discount codes, files and theme settings, through the permissions the merchant approves at install.

We don't store the names or email addresses of the store's staff. If a merchant emails us for support, we keep that correspondence to help them.

// shoppers

Information about shoppers

Promodal doesn't collect, store or receive personal information about the people who visit a merchant's store.

Results. When a modal is shown, clicked or used to sign up, the storefront tells the app, which adds one to that modal's count for the day. Only the modal and the kind of event are sent: no names, email addresses, cookies or other identifiers, and the app doesn't record shoppers' IP addresses.

Email signups. A signup form in a modal sends the shopper's email address to Shopify's own customer signup, on the store's domain. The shopper becomes a subscriber in the merchant's Shopify customer list, under the merchant's own privacy policy. Promodal never receives or stores the address.

Browser storage. So that a modal shows as often as the merchant chooses (for example, once per visit), Promodal keeps a few small entries in the shopper's own browser (local and session storage, not cookies): when the current visit started, when each modal was last shown, whether the shopper has already signed up, and briefly, that an item was just added to the cart. These stay on the shopper's device, are never sent to us, and can be cleared at any time by clearing the browser's data for the store. They are needed for the modals to work, not for tracking or advertising.

// use

How we use information

  • To run the app: showing modals on the store and in the admin.
  • To show merchants their modals' results.
  • To check the store's plan, so the right features apply.
  • To give support, fix problems and keep the app secure.

We don't use information for advertising, and we don't sell it.

// sharing

Who we share it with

  • Shopify, which runs the platform the app is part of and handles billing for app plans.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs the servers and database the app uses, and only processes data on our behalf.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.
// retention

How long we keep it

We keep a store's data while Promodal is installed. When a merchant uninstalls the app, its access to the store ends straight away, and 48 hours later Shopify asks us to delete the store's data: its modals, results and settings are then removed. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help.

// security

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to the app's servers and database is restricted. Requests from the storefront are checked with Shopify's signatures before the app acts on them.

// your-rights

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete information about you, or to object to how it is used.

  • Merchants can email us at hello@derekgeorge.org, or uninstall the app to have the store's data deleted.
  • Shoppers: Promodal holds no personal information about you. Email signups are held by the store you signed up with, so please contact that store. Requests Shopify passes to us on a shopper's behalf are answered through Shopify.

If you're in the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

// changes

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll let merchants know in the app or by email.
