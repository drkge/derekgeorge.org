Bunty — Support

Last updated 14 September 2026

The guide is inside the app. Open Bunty, go to the Babies tab, tap About Bunty, then How Bunty works.

It is kept there rather than here on purpose. Bunty gets used at 3am, one-handed, sometimes on a phone with no signal — which is the worst possible moment to need a website. Keeping one copy also means there is no second version quietly disagreeing with the first.

// what-it-covers

What the guide covers

  • Logging a feed, a nappy or a sleep, and undoing a mis-tap
  • Editing the time of something logged late
  • Adding formula, breast milk, amounts and a note to a feed
  • How the feed alarm decides when to go off, and why it never records a feed for you
  • Why logging a feed ends a sleep that was running
  • What to say to Siri, and what works with the phone locked
  • What the Trends figures mean — and what they do not
  • Twins, and how one baby is kept apart from another
  • Where the data lives and who can see it
// contact

Getting in touch

Questions, problems, or something Bunty should do and doesn't: apps@derekgeorge.org.

Bunty has no account and no server, so there is nothing to reset and no data for anyone to look up on your behalf. If something has gone wrong, describing what you tapped and what happened is the most useful thing you can send.
