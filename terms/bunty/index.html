Bunty — Terms & Disclaimer

Last updated 8 September 2026

Bunty is a notebook, not a nurse. It records what you type in and shows it back to you. It does not interpret anything, it knows nothing about your baby, and it is not qualified to tell you whether they are feeding, sleeping or growing as they should.

Your midwife, health visitor, GP or another medical professional is the authority — not this app. If you are worried about your baby, speak to them. Never delay seeking advice, and never discount advice you have been given, because of anything Bunty shows you.

// what-the-numbers-mean

What the numbers actually mean

The Trends screen counts and averages what you have logged — how many feeds, wet nappies, dirty nappies and sleeps a day, how long between feeds, and how long the sleeps lasted. Every one of them describes your own log over the days you recorded. They are not targets, they are not compared against any guideline, and they say nothing about what is normal or healthy for a baby of any age.

A low number usually means a busy day when logging slipped, not a problem. A missed entry or a mistyped time changes the figures, and nothing in the app is verified or corrected.

Sleeps are worth a particular word. Logging a feed or a nappy change ends a sleep that was running, because a baby being fed or changed is awake. That saves you a tap, but it means a sleep ends when you record the next thing, not when your baby actually stirred — so if you tend to log a few minutes after the fact, your sleeps will read slightly long.

// not-a-medical-device

Not a medical device

Bunty does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent anything, and makes no clinical claims. It has no regulatory clearance and needs none, because it is a record-keeping tool. It measures nothing on its own — every figure comes from you tapping a button.

// alarms

Alarms are a convenience, not a safety system

The optional feed alarm is there to be helpful, and it can fail. It will not reach you if the phone is off, out of battery, or if iOS declines to deliver it for any reason. Please do not let anything about your baby's welfare depend on it.
